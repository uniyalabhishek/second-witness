# Reach

## Who is it for, and would they use it?

Multisig and treasury signers, starting with Safe. They're few, they move a lot of money, and they already follow a pre-signing routine. It costs them one button and asks nothing of the site. Site teams can also watch the [feed](https://second-witness.vercel.app/feed/) for changes to their own files.

## Can they try it today?

Yes. Load the [extension](https://second-witness.vercel.app/extension) and press the button on [app.safe.global](https://app.safe.global), [swap.cow.fi](https://swap.cow.fi), [revoke.cash](https://revoke.cash) or the [demo](https://second-witness.vercel.app/demo). Witnesses run in public [GitHub Actions](../.github/workflows/witness.yml).

## Will we keep building it after Mumbai?

Yes: more listed sites, a witness run by someone else, and the Chrome Web Store.
