# Reach

## Who is it for, and would they use it?

Multisig and treasury signers, starting with Safe. They're few, they move a lot of money, and they already follow a pre-signing routine. Bybit's signers lost about $1.5B to altered code in a page they trusted, and nothing in their browser could tell them. The cost to use this is one button, and it asks nothing of the site. Site teams can also watch the [feed](DEMO_URL/feed/) for changes to their own files.

## Can they try it today?

Yes. Load the [extension zip](DEMO_URL/second-witness-extension.zip) and press the button on [app.safe.global](https://app.safe.global), [swap.cow.fi](https://swap.cow.fi), [revoke.cash](https://revoke.cash) or the [Demo Treasury](DEMO_URL/). Witnesses are running on the live sites now, and anyone can audit them in [GitHub Actions](../.github/workflows/witness.yml).

## Will we keep building it after Mumbai?

Yes, on this order: more listed sites, a witness run by someone other than us, and the Chrome Web Store. Arkiv mainnet is what would let the records carry real weight.
